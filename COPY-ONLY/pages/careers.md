# Careers at Hydrilla

Hydrilla AI is a small team building an AI 3D workspace for artists and studios: meshes, materials, exports, and the software around them.

The work sits where research meets production. Every feature has to survive contact with a real pipeline, which means engineers, researchers, and artists review the same results and hold them to the same standard.

**[Section 2: Who we hire]**

## Who we hire

We hire people who care about craft on both sides of the screen: those who write the software and those who judge the 3D it produces.

| Team | What you work on |
|---|---|
| Engineering | Full-stack product, 3D runtime, and generation infrastructure. |
| 3D and research | Model quality, mesh structure, materials, and evaluation for BlueFox, Hydrilla's 3D model. |

**[Section 3: What we look for]**

## What we look for

Show us work you are proud of, whether that is a shipped product, a research project, a tool you built for yourself, or a portfolio of 3D. We care more about what you have made and how you think about it than about titles.

**[Section 4: How to apply]**

## How to apply

Email founders@hydrilla.ai with the subject "Careers / your name", links to your work, and your location, even if no role fits yet. Meet the [team →](/team) or read [about Hydrilla →](/about) first.
