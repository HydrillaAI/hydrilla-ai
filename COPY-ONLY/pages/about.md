# About Hydrilla

Hydrilla AI is an AI 3D workspace for studio and artist pipelines. We build the Hydrilla workspace and BlueFox, Hydrilla's 3D model. Despite the name, we're a software company, not the aquatic plant.

This page sets out how the company, its research lab, and its products fit together, so it is always clear who builds what. The people behind them are on the team page linked at the end.

**[Section 2: What we build]**

## What we build

Hydrilla is one workspace with three parts. [BlueFox →](/bluefox) is our 3D model, which turns prompts and pictures into segmented models with UVs and textures. [Water Agent →](/water) works through whole sets of assets with artists, from the first brief to the finished pieces. And the [workspace →](/features) ties generation, review, and handoff together in the browser and in your tools.

**[Section 3: Brand family]**

## One company, its research lab, and its model

Hydrilla AI is the product and company; Hawan Research Labs is its research lab. The lab is where our 3D research and experimentation happen, and it builds BlueFox. Read about [Hawan Research Labs →](/research).

## How we work

We build for professional artists and studios, so we aim to be precise about what our tools do and where they stop. Our model's limits are published on the [BlueFox page →](/bluefox#limits), and the [security page →](/security) explains how your work is handled.
