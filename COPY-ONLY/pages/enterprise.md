# Bring AI 3D to your whole studio

Hydrilla AI for whole studios: BlueFox, Hydrilla's 3D model, and Water Agent across your team, in one shared workspace.

When generation moves from one artist's experiment to a studio-wide tool, the questions change. Who has access, how much volume the pipeline needs, how jobs from your own systems are handled, and how your data is treated all need clear answers. Enterprise is where we work those out with you.

## What Enterprise adds for your studio

Enterprise adds seats, API concurrency, custom plans, and pipeline support. Each is shaped around how your studio works rather than a fixed tier.

Card 1 title: Seats
Card 1 line: Art, lookdev, and pipeline staff work together in one shared workspace, so generations, reviews, and approved versions live in the same place.

Card 2 title: API concurrency
Card 2 line: Jobs from your own backend, sized to your volume, for asset libraries, build steps, and internal tools.

Card 3 title: Custom plans
Card 3 line: Credit pools and queue priority set around your monthly volume, instead of individual allowances.

Card 4 title: Pipeline support
Card 4 line: Help connecting the Bridge and plugins to your pipeline, so artists get generation inside the tools they already use.

**[Section 3: Pro vs Enterprise]**

## How this differs from Pro

Pro is built for individuals and small teams who buy online. Enterprise is for studios that need an agreement around their volume and their tools.

| | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| How you buy | Self-serve and monthly, on [pricing →](/pricing) | An agreement shaped around your studio |
| Plans | Monthly credits on a set plan | Custom plans and credit pools |

**[Section 4: Procurement]**

## Answers for procurement

**How is our data handled?**
Your data isn't used to train our models, on any plan. Generated assets are kept to your own workspace, and the [security page →](/security) explains both.

**Can we send a security questionnaire?**
Yes. Send it to founders@hydrilla.ai, naming your studio and when you need answers. We answer in writing with what we can confirm.

**Can we start on Pro and move up later?**
Yes. You can evaluate on any self-serve plan first. When you get in touch, tell us what you run today, and we'll plan Enterprise around it.
