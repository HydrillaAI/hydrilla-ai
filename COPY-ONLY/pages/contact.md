# Contact Hydrilla

Reach the team behind Hydrilla AI about your account, a generation, or press. Pick the route that fits.

Choosing the right route helps us answer faster. Most account and how-to questions are already covered in the docs and FAQ, so check those first; for anything else, write to us and a member of the team will reply.

**[Section 2: Routes]**

## Choose a route

| Route | How |
|---|---|
| Support | Account, billing, or a generation that went wrong: email founders@hydrilla.ai, or check the [FAQ →](/faq) and [docs →](/docs) first. |
| Press | Interviews and logos: write to founders@hydrilla.ai with your outlet and deadline. |
| Sales and demos | Seats, volume, or a walkthrough live on [Enterprise →](/enterprise). |

**[Section 3: What to include]**

## Help us help you

For a generation problem, include your account email, the workspace, and a screenshot of the result. For billing, include the plan you are on. For press, tell us your outlet, your deadline, and what the piece is about.

## Send a message

Form fields: Full name | Work email | Company or studio | Message
