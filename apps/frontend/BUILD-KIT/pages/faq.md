# Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on owning what you make in Hydrilla AI, running your account, and getting help.

**[Section 2: Licensing and ownership]**

## Licensing and ownership

**Do I own the 3D models I generate?**
Hydrilla's Terms of Service cover ownership of the 3D assets you generate. The [ownership section of the Terms →](/terms#ownership) has the exact wording.

**Can I upload reference images I didn't create?**
Only upload images you have the right to use. The [Terms →](/terms) apply to everything you upload.

**[Section 3: Accounts and data]**

## Accounts and data

**How do I sign in?**
You sign in to Studio with your Hydrilla account. How each plugin signs in is listed on [integrations →](/integrations#sign-in). [Sign-in security →](/security)

**Can I delete my work or my account?**
To close your account or ask for its data to be deleted, write to founders@hydrilla.ai from your account email.

**Where can I read how my data is handled?**
The [Privacy Policy →](/privacy) covers personal data. The [security page →](/security) covers model training, accounts, stored assets, and exports for studio reviewers.

**[Section 4: Support]**

## Support

**How do I get help?**
Email founders@hydrilla.ai or use the [contact form →](/contact). For a generation problem, include your account email, the workspace, and a screenshot.

**Where should I start if I'm new?**
Follow the steps in the [docs →](/docs), from sign-up to a first import in your own tools.
