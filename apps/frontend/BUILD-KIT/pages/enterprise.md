**[Section 4: Procurement]**

## Answers for procurement

**How is our data handled?**
Your data isn't used to train our models, on any plan. Generated assets are kept to your own workspace, and the [security page →](/security) explains both.

**Can we send a security questionnaire?**
Yes. Send it to founders@hydrilla.ai, naming your studio and when you need answers. We answer in writing with what we can confirm.
