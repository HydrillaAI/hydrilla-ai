- 6+ Native 3D plugins: generate and receive models in Blender, Maya, 3ds Max, Unity, Unreal, and Godot.

**[Section 4: Workflow & Controls]**

## Pro control over workflow

Every artist, technical director, and automation script works against the same studio workspace and credit pool: Create as one team, Direct with Water Agent, Connect your 3D stack, and Automate via API & MCP.

## All the 3D pipeline controls you rely on

Every model arrives segmented into editable parts with unwrapped UVs and attached PBR texture maps, ready for lighting, material swaps, and scene assembly.

## Your data and IP are protected

- No training on your data: Hydrilla never trains models on your prompts, references, or generated 3D meshes, protecting unreleased titles and client briefs.
- Centralized seats & credit pool: manage studio members in one workspace and share one unified monthly credit pool across artists, plugins, and API builds.
- Full ownership & vendor reviews: you own generated 3D outputs for commercial release, and our team supports security questionnaires and legal terms.
