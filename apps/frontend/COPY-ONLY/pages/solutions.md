# Hydrilla AI 3D by industry

Five ways teams put BlueFox, Hydrilla's 3D model, to work in Hydrilla AI, each with its fit, limits, and tools.

Every industry asks something different of a 3D asset. A game prop has a polygon budget, a previs set piece has a camera distance, a furnished room has a client review, and a product model has a spec sheet. Each page below starts from those demands and is honest about where generation helps and where an artist still leads.
