## How we compare

Every row comes from each company's public pages, dated when we checked. We give no scores, imply no relationship, and ask you to confirm details on each product's own site. Products change quickly, so each page carries the date of its last check, and we update the rows when we find a change.

## Test in your own scene

A table tells you what a tool offers. Only your own scene tells you how the output behaves under your lighting, in your engine, against your budget. The [buying guide →](/compare/how-to-choose) sets out a fair way to run that test with any tool.
